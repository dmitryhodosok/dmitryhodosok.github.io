ApartyRadar

Side project · solo · apartyradar.pl

One search for every apartment and room for rent in Poland. Good flats in Warsaw fill their viewing slots within about 30 minutes of being posted; ApartyRadar collects listings live from nine sources, uses AI to pull the real monthly cost out of Polish descriptions, and sends a Telegram alert within minutes.

Snapshot from November 2026: Warsaw 33.5k, Kraków 15.7k, Wrocław 9.9k, Gdańsk 6.7k, Poznań 5.4k, Łódź 4.4k.

What it does

  • Aggregates Otodom, OLX, Gratka, Nieruchomości Online, GetHome, Domiporta and Adresowo, plus Facebook groups and Telegram channels.
  • AI extracts the hidden admin fee and utilities, deposit, pet rules and lease type, so a “3,000 zł” flat shows its real price.
  • Merges duplicate agency listings and filters to owners only.
  • Rent and buy search, price‑per‑m² analytics by district, and a /100 neighbourhood score.
  • Full interface in Polish, English, Ukrainian and Russian, built for people moving to Poland.

How I built and run it

  • Scrapers on a Hetzner VPS under systemd, exposed to Claude through a Cloudflare tunnel and an MCP connector.
  • AI extraction on a pooled roster of free‑tier LLM providers with fast and slow lanes and per‑provider daily limits.
  • Three surfaces: the web app, the @aparty_pl_bot Telegram bot, and an Android app.
  • Growth through community replies to apartment‑seekers on Threads; free forever for renters, monetised through relocation‑service partnerships.

OfertyRadar

Side project · solo · in beta · ofertyradar.pl

The same idea applied to jobs: one search across Poland's job boards, built for people whose Polish isn't perfect yet. Browse without an account, sign in with Telegram to save a search, and get new matching vacancies as Telegram messages.

What it does

  • Aggregates Pracuj.pl, OLX, Just Join IT, RocketJobs and selected public Telegram channels.
  • Filters by the language the posting is written in, work mode, city and salary in PLN.
  • Matching is word‑prefix, case‑ and diacritic‑insensitive, and handles Cyrillic, so “менеджер” and “kierownik” searches both work.
  • Interface in Polish, English, Russian and Ukrainian.

How I built and run it

  • Sources are crawled roughly every 15 minutes; alerts go out only for postings that are new since the search was saved.
  • Telegram login and bot commands to pause or resume all alerts.
  • Started from a job‑board scraping and LLM‑matching pipeline I built earlier, extended with a Jooble API integration and ATS‑feed discovery.
